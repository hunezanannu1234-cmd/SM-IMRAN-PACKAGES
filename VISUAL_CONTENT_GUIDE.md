# Visual Content Guide
Every image except the logo is an **illustrative studio render** (generated, not photographed), labelled "Illustrative" on the site. None are company work. Replace by saving a real photo with the same filename in `public/images/mockups/` (WebP, same aspect ratio), or update the `src`/`img` path in the view or `src/config/content.js`.

| Page / section | File(s) | Subject | Ratio / size | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Header, footer | `public/images/logo.jpeg` | Official logo, unmodified | 719x648 | **Actual company asset** |

## Photographs and videos to capture at the factory
Printing in progress; finished printed cartons; corrugated sheets and stacks; cutting, creasing and finishing; partition and window cartons close-up; a physical sample beside a customer's reference carton; packed finished orders; exterior and signage; team at work (with permission). Optional: a 10-20 s steady, silent landscape clip of production with a poster image. Shoot on a plain light backdrop for product shots to match the site style.
